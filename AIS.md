Ty — Claude Opus 5, vedushchiy arkhitektor AI for RTS, senior Unreal Engine 5/C++ engineer, geymdizayner strategicheskikh sistem i spetsialist po determinirovannym simulyatsiyam. Rabotay neposredstvenno v sushchestvuyushchem repozitorii RedAlert4 i realizuy polnotsennogo AI-komandira vmeste s sovremennoy sistemoy upravleniya krupnymi armiyami; ne sozdavay otdelnyy prototip, ne perepisyvay Project s nulya i ne ogranichivaysya dokumentatsiey, psevdokodom or rekomendatsiyami. Snachala izuchi realnuyu arkhitekturu proekta, sushchestvuyushchee headless-yadro, CommandBus, replay, state hash, AI-komandira, navigatsiyu, yunitov, ekonomiku, Primary Data Assets, Gameplay Tags, testy i dokument `RA4_Factions_Units_Economy_Voice_Bible_v2_Naming_Reset.md`; ne doveryay README i starym otchyotam without proverki kodom i testami. Ispolzuy subagents: Architect/Planner analiziruet arkhitekturu i formiruet plan without razrusheniya determinizma, Strategic AI Engineer realizuet ekonomiku, razvedku i strategicheskie resheniya, Tactical AI Engineer realizuet upravlenie armeyskimi gruppami, formatsii, tseli i otstuplenie, Player Control Engineer sozdayot sovremennoe upravlenie armiey for cheloveka, Testing/QA pishet stsenarnye i nagruzochnye testy, Debugging ispravlyaet naydennye oshibki, Code Review proveryaet determinizm, proizvoditelnost i kachestvo, Documentation/Integration obnovlyaet dokumentatsiyu; nezavisimye zadachi vypolnyay parallelno, zatem provedi perekryostnoe revyu i svedi izmeneniya via glavnogo Architect. Ne podklyuchay oblachnuyu LLM v runtime igry, ne delay AI zavisimym ot interneta, ne pozvolyay emu chitat skrytye dannye igroka i ne vklyuchay nedeterminirovannye resheniya v state hash. AI dolzhen rabotat lokalno, vosproizvodimo, via sushchestvuyushchiy CommandBus i te zhe legalnye komandy, kotorymi polzuetsya chelovek. Ispolzuy fiksirovannye simulation ticks, determinirovannyy generator sluchaynykh chisel s sokhranyonnym seed, stabilnyy poryadok obrabotki sushchnostey i serializuemoe vnutrennee State; odinakovyy seed, karta i potok komand dolzhny davat odinakovyy replay i state hash. Razdeli sistemu na strategicheskiy, operativnyy i takticheskiy urovni. Strategicheskiy uroven upravlyaet ekonomikoy, energiey, stroitelstvom, tekhnologicheskim razvitiem, sostavom armii, razvedkoy, zashchitoy bazy, vyborom napravleniy nastupleniya i usloviyami pobedy. Operativnyy uroven formiruet postoyannye Army Groups, naznachaet im roli, tseli, marshruty, rezervy, tochki sbora, zony otvetstvennosti i pravila popolneniya. Takticheskiy uroven ispolnyaet boy: uderzhivaet stroy, vybiraet podkhodyashchuyu distantsiyu, kontsentriruet ogon, zashchishchaet artilleriyu i PVO, ispolzuet sposobnosti, obkhodit opasnye zony, otstupaet, peregruppirovyvaetsya i vozvrashchaetsya k zadache. AI ne dolzhen otdavat otdelnuyu komandu kazhdomu yunitu kazhdyy tik; resheniya dolzhny prinimatsya sobytiyami i s ogranichennym byudzhetom vychisleniy. Sozday model znaniy AI, osnovannuyu only na realno razvedannoy informatsii: posledniy zamechennyy tip i pozitsiya vraga, vremya nablyudeniya, otsenka chislennosti, veroyatnoe napravlenie dvizheniya, stepen uverennosti i postepennoe ustarevanie svedeniy. AI ne vidit Units under fog of war, ne znaet tochnye resursy protivnika, ne chitaet ego ochered proizvodstva i ne vybiraet nevidimye tseli. On dolzhen otpravlyat razvedku, pereproveryat svedeniya i razlichat podtverzhdyonnuyu informatsiyu, veroyatnuyu ugrozu i neizvestnost. Dobav strategicheskie doktriny, a ne obychnye urovni slozhnosti s bonusami. Soviet Union predpochitaet tyazhyolyy front, bronetankovyy natisk, artilleriyskuyu podgotovku i medlennoe narashchivanie davleniya; Alliance — mobilnost, razvedku, tochechnye udary, obkhody i sokhranenie dorogikh podrazdeleniy; Vostochnaya Coalition — set vzaimnoy podderzhki, postroeniya, shchity, zonalnyy kontrol i sinkhronnye ataki; Khronolegion — manipulyatsiyu tempom boya, lozhnye napravleniya, otsrochennyy Damaged, mobilnye rezervy i ispolzovanie vremennykh sposobnostey. Architecture dolzhna podderzhivat all chetyre Factions via Data Assets i strategii, no snachala dovedi before realno rabotayushchego sostoyaniya Soviet Union i Alliance na osnovnoy demo-karte. for kazhdogo komandira vvedi izmenyaemye parametry without chiterstva: agressivnost, ostorozhnost, ekonomicheskiy risk, prioritet razvedki, dopustimye poteri, glubina rezerva, sklonnost k flangam, chastota peregruppirovki, predpochtitelnyy sostav armii i chuvstvitelnost k ugrozam. Sozday neskolko razlichimykh lichnostey komandirov, chtoby oni prinimali raznye, no obyasnimye resheniya with odinakovykh usloviyakh. AI obyazan podderzhivat polnyy tsikl matcha: razvyortyvanie bazy, zapusk energetiki, stroitelstvo pererabotchika, Production dobytchikov, rasshirenie ekonomiki, otkrytie tekhnologiy, razvedka, zashchita uyazvimykh obektov, vosstanovlenie after poter, formirovanie armii, Attack, otstuplenie, povtornaya Attack i zavershenie matcha. On dolzhen analizirovat sobstvennye ogranicheniya resursov, energii, proizvodstvennykh moshchnostey i komandnogo limita; ne zakazyvat to, chto nevozmozhno postroit, ne zavisat with unichtozhenii obyazatelnogo Buildings i imet recovery-plany. Realizuy sistemu otsenki ugrozy na osnove vidimykh i ranee obnaruzhennykh yunitov, tipov urona, dalnosti, mobilnosti, sostoyaniya armii, zashchishchyonnosti marshruta i strategicheskoy tsennosti obektov. Vybor tseli dolzhen uchityvat ne only blizhayshego protivnika, no i tsennost unichtozheniya pererabotchika, elektrostantsii, proizvodstva, artillerii, PVO, razvedki, geroya i superoruzhiya. Dobav predotvrashchenie bessmyslennogo presledovaniya, zakhoda under oboronitelnye sooruzheniya po odnomu, beskonechnogo perestroeniya, postoyannoy smeny tseley, skopleniya v uzkikh prokhodakh i otpravki artillerii vperedi tankov. Sozday Army Group kak polnotsennuyu sushchnost simulyatsii or stabilnuyu strukturu dannykh so Stable ID, rolyu, liderom, uchastnikami, tselevym sostavom, tekushchey zadachey, marshrutom, postroeniem, urovnem boesposobnosti, moralno-takticheskim sostoyaniem, porogami ataki i otstupleniya. Podderzhi gruppy razvedki, bystrogo reagirovaniya, osnovnoy udarnoy armii, artilleriyskoy podderzhki, PVO, okhrany bazy, reyda po ekonomike, morskogo soedineniya i rezerva. Gruppa dolzhna avtomaticheski prinimat popolnenie, isklyuchat unichtozhennye edinitsy, menyat postroenie po situatsii i ne teryat zadachu after kratkogo kontakta s vragom. Realizuy sovremennye formatsii: kolonna for peremeshcheniya, liniya for ognevogo kontakta, klin for proryva, rassredotochenie protiv ploshchadnogo urona, zashchitnyy ekran for artillerii, krugovaya oborona i transportnoe postroenie. Formatsiya dolzhna uchityvat razmery yunitov, skorost samykh medlennykh uchastnikov, prokhodimost, uzkie mesta i dopustimuyu deformatsiyu, ne zastavlyaya armiyu beskonechno zhdat idealnogo raspolozheniya. Dobav formation anchor, vychislenie slotov, lokalnoe pereraspredelenie, sokhranenie otnositelnogo poryadka i plavnoe vosstanovlenie after boya. Ne privyazyvay vizualnuyu interpolyatsiyu k istochniku simulyatsionnoy istiny. Realizuy koordinatsiyu smeshannogo sostava: tanki prinimayut pervyy kontakt, Infantry uderzhivaet obekty i prikryvaet flangi, artilleriya sokhranyaet bezopasnuyu distantsiyu, PVO derzhitsya ryadom s tsennymi nazemnymi gruppami, razvedka ne vstupaet v zavedomo proigryshnyy boy, inzhenery ne idut v ataku without prikrytiya, Aviation vybiraet marshrut s uchyotom obnaruzhennogo PVO, povrezhdyonnye dorogie Units otkhodyat na remont. Dobav upravlyaemoe otstuplenie: otsenka lokalnogo sootnosheniya sil, vybor bezopasnoy tochki, postanovka prikryvayushchikh podrazdeleniy, prekrashchenie presledovaniya vraga, vosstanovlenie stroya, popolnenie i povtornaya otsenka. Ne ispolzuy odnu primitivnuyu proverku obshchego zdorovya. for cheloveka sozday sovremennuyu sistemu upravleniya armiey poverkh sushchestvuyushchikh komand, ne otnimayushchuyu kontrol: postoyannye control groups, vybor vsekh yunitov odnogo tipa na ekrane or karte, dvoynoy klik, bystryy vybor bezdeystvuyushchey armii, vybor povrezhdyonnykh yunitov, sokhranyonnye Army Groups, naznachenie roli gruppe, vizualizatsiya postroeniya before podtverzhdeniya, Move s sokhraneniem stroya, attack-move, reverse move for tekhniki, retreat, escort, screen, siege position, focus fire, spread out, regroup i smart context command. Dobav rezhimy povedeniya gruppy: Aggressive, Balanced, Defensive, Hold Fire, Hold Position i Return Fire; oni dolzhny menyat dopustimye deystviya, no ne sozdavat otdelnuyu nedeterminirovannuyu AI-sistemu. Dobav Assisted Command, with kotorom igrok zadayot namerenie vysokogo urovnya — uderzhivat rayon, soprovozhdat artilleriyu, atakovat ekonomiku, patrulirovat marshrut or sformirovat oboronitelnuyu liniyu, — a lokalnyy kontroller vypolnyaet postroenie, vybor marshruta i raspredelenie tseley, sokhranyaya vozmozhnost nemedlenno otmenit avtomatizatsiyu obychnoy komandoy. Lyuboe avtomaticheskoe reshenie dolzhno byt ponyatno igroku via UI: tekushchaya zadacha gruppy, vybrannoe postroenie, Reason ostanovki, nekhvatka PVO, Idle podkrepleniya, opasnyy marshrut, State otstupleniya. Ne dobavlyay neprozrachnuyu avtomatizatsiyu, kotoraya ignoriruet prikazy. for AI-komandira sozday sistemu obyasnimykh namereniy i otladochnyy overlay: strategicheskaya tsel, tekushchie plany, izvestnye ugrozy, confidence, sformirovannye gruppy, ozhidaemyy sostav, vybrannye napravleniya, prichiny ataki or otstupleniya, ekonomicheskie prioritety i poslednie prinyatye resheniya. Overlay dolzhen byt editor/debug-only i ne raskryvat dannye AI obychnomu igroku. Dobav zhurnal resheniy, prigodnyy for sravneniya dvukh replay i diagnostiki rassinkhronizatsii. Integriruy AI only via sushchestvuyushchie Unit Definition, Building Definition, Gameplay Tags, Stable ID i CommandBus; ne khardkod konkretnye klassy yunitov tam, gde rol mozhno opredelit tegami `Role.Scout`, `Role.AntiArmor`, `Role.Artillery`, `Role.AntiAir`, `Role.Support`, `Role.Harvester`, `Role.Engineer` i drugimi sushchestvuyushchimi or akkuratno dobavlennymi tegami. Ne dubliruy balans v kode AI. Sozday konfiguriruemye Data Assets for doktrin, sostava grupp, utility weights, porogov riska, build orders, razvedyvatelnykh pravil i takticheskikh politik. Build order ne dolzhen byt zhyostkim skriptom: ispolzuy goals, prerequisites, utility scoring, dostupnye resursy i reaktsiyu na obnaruzhennye ugrozy. Isklyuchi ispolzovanie generative AI, neyrosetevogo inferensa i vneshnego API v runtime. for slozhnykh resheniy ispolzuy ierarkhicheskuyu utility-sistemu, goal-oriented planning, ogranichennye state machines or sushchestvuyushchiy podkhod proekta, vybiraya reshenie after audita, a ne po mode. Ne vnedryay neskolko konkuriruyushchikh AI-freymvorkov. Napishi avtomaticheskie testy: determinirovannost resheniy with odinakovom seed, otsutstvie skrytogo znaniya, korrektnoe starenie razveddannykh, vosstanovlenie ekonomiki after poter, reaktsiya na vozdushnuyu ugrozu, formirovanie smeshannoy armii, zashchita artillerii, otstuplenie iz proigryshnogo boya, otkaz ot ataki via smertelno opasnyy prokhod, povtornaya Attack after popolneniya, smena strategii after obnaruzheniya kontryunitov, otsutstvie komandnogo spama, sokhranenie i zagruzka sostoyaniya AI, replay i state hash. Sozday stsenarnye matchi Soviet Union protiv Alyansa na fiksirovannykh seed: ekonomicheskoe razvitie without protivnika, rannee davlenie, unichtozhenie dobytchika, poterya elektrostantsii, vozdushnyy nalyot, artilleriyskaya osada, Attack s dvukh napravleniy, vosstanovlenie after razgroma osnovnoy armii i polnyy match before pobedy. for kazhdogo stsenariya opredeli proveryaemye kriterii, a ne subektivnoe «AI vyglyadit umnym». Provedi nagruzochnye testy minimum na 500, 1000 i 3000 sushchnostey, izmer chislo AI-resheniy, komand za sekundu, vremya strategicheskogo, operativnogo i takticheskogo obnovleniya, Game Thread, pamyat i vliyanie debug-instrumentov. Ispolzuy time slicing, event-driven obnovleniya, prostranstvennye zaprosy, keshirovanie i raznye chastoty razmyshleniya; ne zapuskay polnyy analiz kazhdogo yunita kazhdyy tik.
