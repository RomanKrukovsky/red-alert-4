# Report o kastinge sistemnykh golosov EVA (EVA Voice Casting Report) — Red Alert 4

## 1. Introduction i kontseptsiya kastinga

V ramkakh pervogo etapa proizvodstva sistemnoy ozvuchki (Phase 1 EVA Voice Production) for Red Alert 4 razrabotan originalnyy paket sistemnykh golosov EVA. for kazhdoy iz 4 fraktsiy sozdan unikalnyy golosovoy profil, ne zaimstvuyushchiy maneru, intonatsii or frazirovku iz predydushchikh igr serii Command & Conquer / Red Alert or storonnikh media-franshiz.

Kazhdaya fraktsiya proshla etap alternativnogo kastinga s zapisyu i proverkoy **6 originalnykh kandidatov Voice Design (C1–C6)**.
