# Modern Warfare RTS — Concept & Future Plans

This document outlines the design concept, faction matrix, and gameplay mechanics for a modern geopolitical Real-Time Strategy (RTS) setting powered by Unreal Engine 5.

---

## 1. Core Principles & Setting

* **Setting:** Modern multi-polar geopolitical warfare (Hybrid warfare, EW, FPV drones, cyber ops, precision strike, supply logistics).
* **Engine & Graphics:** Unreal Engine 5 (Nanite, Lumen, Substrate materials, Chaos destruction, dynamic weather).
* **Pacing:** High-intensity tactical RTS (combining the speed of *C&C Generals* / *Act of Aggression* with the tactical depth of *World in Conflict*).

---

## 2. Global Factions & Doctrines

### Core Powers
1. **United States (USA / NATO Cyber Command)**
   * *Focus:* High-tech dominance, stealth aviation (F-35, B-21), satellite surveillance, autonomous loyal wingman drones.
2. **Russia (Eurasian Defense Initiative)**
   * *Focus:* Layered anti-air (S-400/S-500), Electronic Warfare (EW jamming of minimaps/drones), heavy armor (T-90M, T-14), thermobaric artillery (TOS-1A).
3. **China (PLA / Pan-Asian Federation)**
   * *Focus:* Industrial scale, mass production, hypersonic missile containers, quantum C2 networks, cheap drone swarms.
4. **European Union (EU Force)**
   * *Focus:* Modular vehicle platforms (Boxer, Caesar), Skynex laser air defense, elite sniper teams, precision strikes.
5. **Japan (JSDF / High-Tech Cyber-Dome)**
   * *Focus:* AI-driven automation, unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs), microwave missile defense, light walking drones.

### Regional & Special Power Blocks
6. **India (South Asian Federation)**
   * *Focus:* Supersonic BrahMos cruise missiles, mountain infantry, mixed hybrid tech arsenal.
7. **Israel (Levantine Defense Directorate)**
   * *Focus:* Trophy Active Protection Systems (APS), Merkava IV tanks, Iron Dome short-range defense, urban warfare specialization.
8. **Iran (Middle Eastern Alliance)**
   * *Focus:* Underground tunnel networks, long-range cheap loitering munitions (Shahed), ballistic artillery.
9. **Turkey (Anatolian Defense Complex)**
   * *Focus:* MALE combat UAVs (Bayraktar, Kızılelma), mobile MLRS, fast wheeled armored vehicles (Kirpi).
10. **South Korea (ROK Shield)**
    * *Focus:* Auto-counter-battery artillery (K9 Thunder), automated perimeter defense, hyper-automated logistics.
11. **Nordic Coalition (Sweden, Norway, Finland)**
    * *Focus:* Deep forest/snow stealth, shoot-and-scoot artillery (Archer), CV90 IFVs, Gripen multirole jets.
12. **United Kingdom & Commonwealth**
    * *Focus:* Long-range cyber attacks, SAS covert ops, Royal Marines, Challenger 3 armor.

### Exotic & Specialized Factions
13. **Switzerland (Alpine Redoubt)**
    * *Focus:* Financial blockade mechanics (freezing enemy income), underground retractable mountain fortresses, Oerlikon CIWS.
14. **Singapore (Island Smart Nation)**
    * *Focus:* Total map visibility via Smart Nation surveillance grid, automated urban laser defense, 100% automated combat units.
15. **Mexico / Syndicate (Hybrid Fed & Cartel)**
    * *Focus:* Improvised armored "Narco-tanks", bribery mechanics ("Plata o Plomo"), tunnel networks, improvised explosive drones.
16. **North Korea (Democratic People's Front)**
    * *Focus:* Massed rocket artillery (10,000 MLRS barrage), propaganda towers (buffing friendly morale / debuffing enemy), underground infantry staging.
17. **UAE / Saudi Dynasties (Gulf PMCs)**
    * *Focus:* Infinite capital, instant PMC contractor deployment, hypercar strike buggies, buyouts of damaged enemy vehicles.
18. **Kazakhstan (Central Asian Nomad Corps)**
    * *Focus:* Steppe speed bonuses (+50% vehicle speed on roads/plains), Baikonur satellite & space debris strikes.

## 3. Key Gameplay Mechanics

* **FPV & Recon Drones:** Players deploy FPV suicide drones and recon quadcopters to spot beyond the fog of war and guide precision artillery.
* **Electronic Warfare (EW):** Jamming trucks generate anti-drone domes, disabling GPS-guided missiles and blinding enemy minimaps.
* **Supply Logistics:** Tanks and artillery require ammunition trucks. Out-of-ammo units lose combat capability.
* **Chaos Destruction:** Urban buildings can be collapsed to block choke points and alter terrain dynamically.
