## 1. Red Star Union (RSU)

### Strategic Fantasy
Heavy industrial juggernaut utilizing kinetic firepower, mass production, heavy armor plating, Tesla weapon technology, and overwhelming frontline force.

### Economic Engine & Construction
- **Economic Engine**: **High-Capacity Heavy Harvester**. Single harvester carries 750 credits of Aethelite. Slower movement, but equipped with heavy armor and a top-mounted defense turret.
- **Construction Mechanics**: **Standard Crane Construction**. Structure footprints are built directly on adjacent friendly ground. Multiple cranes increase base production queue speed.

### Unique Mechanics & Superweapon
- **Faction Resource**: **Mobilization Gauge**. Accrues as RSU units take and deal damage. Reaching 100% enables global "Overdrive Production" (+50% build speed for 45s).
- **Superweapon**: **Tesla Orbital Pulse Cannon**. Calls down a high-voltage atmospheric lightning beam that vaporizes enemy armored columns and disables power grids in a wide radius.

### Unit Roster Summary (Selected Key Units)
1. **RSU Conscript (Infantry)**: Cost: 100 credits. Low-cost frontline rifleman with Molotov grenade secondary ability.
2. **RSU Vanguard Shock Trooper (Infantry)**: Cost: 300 credits. Armored Tesla-rifle trooper effective against vehicles.
3. **RSU General Morozova (Hero)**: Cost: 1200 credits. Heroic infantry commander equipped with heavy armor, call-in artillery strike, and area morale buff.
4. **RSU Hammer Heavy Tank (Vehicle)**: Cost: 900 credits. Twin-cannon medium battle tank with leech beam secondary weapon to siphon enemy health.
5. **RSU Mammoth Siege Engine (Vehicle)**: Cost: 1800 credits. Quad-track heavy assault tank equipped with long-range cannons and anti-air missile pods.
6. **RSU Dreadnought Leviathan (Aircraft)**: Cost: 2200 credits. Armored atmospheric airship firing long-range explosive siege torpedoes.

## 2. Global Defense Coalition (GDC)

### Strategic Fantasy
High-tech allied coalition utilizing precision optics, air superiority, rapid deployment, energy shields, and superior tactical mobility.

### Economic Engine & Construction
- **Economic Engine**: **Aero-Harvester**. High-speed hover harvester that can cross water and cliffs. Lower capacity (350 credits), but immune to terrain obstacles.
- **Construction Mechanics**: **Prefabricated Deployment**. Structures are queued in UI and deployed instantly anywhere within friendly base power grid radius upon completion.

### Unique Mechanics & Superweapon
- **Faction Resource**: **Tactical Intelligence**. Accrues through active radar sweeps and scout unit vision. Unlocks satellite strikes and precision fog-of-war reveals.
- **Superweapon**: **Particle Particle Beam Satellite**. Orbital satellite weapon emitting a continuous surgical laser beam controlled by the player for 15 seconds.

### Unit Roster Summary (Selected Key Units)
1. **GDC Enforcer (Infantry)**: Cost: 150 credits. Precision burst rifleman with deployable sandbag cover ability.
2. **GDC Javelin Specialist (Infantry)**: Cost: 350 credits. Anti-armor and anti-air missile trooper with lock-on laser guidance.
3. **GDC Commander Hart (Hero)**: Cost: 1200 credits. Tactical hero equipped with stealth cloak, high-frequency sniper rifle, and satellite target painter.
4. **GDC Guardian Main Battle Tank (Vehicle)**: Cost: 800 credits. Agile MBT equipped with target painter laser increasing incoming team damage by 20%.
5. **GDC Mirage Cloak Tank (Vehicle)**: Cost: 1100 credits. Thermal stealth tank that disguises itself as natural terrain objects when stationary.
6. **GDC Falcon VTOL Gunship (Aircraft)**: Cost: 1200 credits. High-speed VTOL aircraft with dual chain-guns and air-to-ground rockets.

## 3. Pan-Asian Syndicate (PAS)

### Strategic Fantasy
Advanced cybernetic swarm coalition utilizing subterranean movement, automated drone swarms, stealth infiltration, and resource recycling.

### Economic Engine & Construction
- **Economic Engine**: **Subterranean Drone Collector**. Automated subterranean drill harvester that burrows under terrain to reach distant ore fields safely.
- **Construction Mechanics**: **Nanite Nanofabricator Swarm**. Construction nanites build structures over time. Multiple nanite drones can assist to accelerate construction speed.

### Unique Mechanics & Superweapon
- **Faction Resource**: **Swarm Processing Power**. Dynamic network bandwidth generated by Nodes. Higher bandwidth increases drone reaction speed and weapon reload rates.
- **Superweapon**: **Kinetic Cataclysm Rod**. Drops an tungsten kinetic strike rod from orbit, causing a massive tectonic shockwave that demolishes enemy structures.

### Unit Roster Summary (Selected Key Units)
1. **PAS Infiltrator (Infantry)**: Cost: 200 credits. Cybernetic scout with optical camo and building sabotage abilities.
2. **PAS Mei (Hero)**: Cost: 1200 credits. Cybernetic martial hero who hacks enemy vehicles and redirects incoming missile fire.
3. **PAS Mantis Swarm Drone (Vehicle)**: Cost: 400 credits. Low-cost automated hover drone that attacks in coordinated swarms.
4. **PAS Wave-Cannon Artillery (Vehicle)**: Cost: 1400 credits. Long-range sonic artillery destroying multiple targets in a linear wave path.

## 4. Temporal Resonance Order (TRO)

### Strategic Fantasy
Enigmatic faction exploiting quantum resonance, phase shifting, time manipulation, and spatial teleportation.

### Economic Engine & Construction
- **Economic Engine**: **Temporal Quantum Transposer**. Instantly teleports Aethelite crystals from ore nodes directly into the Refinery, bypassing physical transit.
- **Construction Mechanics**: **Chronos Spatial Phasing**. Structures phase into reality from quantum space. Power failure causes structures to phase out temporarily (invulnerable but inactive).

### Unique Mechanics & Superweapon
- **Faction Resource**: **Temporal Stability Index**. Regenerates over time. Expended to activate global chronal abilities (Recall Army, Temporal Rewind).
- **Superweapon**: **Chronos Stasis Rift Generator**. Opens a localized rift that freezes all entities (friendly and enemy) in time for 30 seconds.

### Unit Roster Summary (Selected Key Units)
1. **TRO Phase Trooper (Infantry)**: Cost: 250 credits. Equipped with temporal beam rifle that slows and eventually freezes targets in stasis.
2. **TRO Voss (Hero)**: Cost: 1400 credits. Master of time who can reverse damage taken by nearby friendly units over the last 5 seconds.
3. **TRO Chrono Tank (Vehicle)**: Cost: 1200 credits. Teleporting light tank capable of blinking across cliffs and obstacles.
4. **TRO Tempest Leviathan (Aircraft)**: Cost: 2000 credits. Heavy phase warship emitting temporal disruption fields that freeze enemy turrets.
